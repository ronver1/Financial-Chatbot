For tax year 2026, the IRS annual contribution limits are as follows. The IRA limit (traditional and Roth combined) is $7,500 for those under age 50, with a catch-up of $1,100 for those 50 and older, for a total of $8,600. The employee contribution limit for a 401(k), 403(b), or most 457(b) plans is $24,500. The 401(k) catch-up for those aged 50 and older is $8,000, for a total of $32,500, and workers aged 60 to 63 qualify for a larger catch-up of $11,250 for a total of $35,750. The combined employee-plus-employer 401(k) limit is $72,000. Health savings account limits are $4,400 for self-only coverage and $8,750 for family coverage, plus a $1,000 catch-up for those aged 55 and older. Note: these figures apply to tax year 2026 and are adjusted by the IRS for inflation each year, so they should be reviewed annually against IRS.gov.
