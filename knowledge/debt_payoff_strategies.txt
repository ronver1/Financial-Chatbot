Two common strategies for paying off multiple debts are the avalanche method and the snowball method. The avalanche method directs all extra payments toward the debt with the highest interest rate first while paying minimums on the rest, then moves to the next highest rate once the first is cleared. It minimizes total interest paid and is mathematically the cheapest approach. The snowball method instead targets the smallest balance first regardless of interest rate, producing quick wins that build motivation, then rolls each freed-up payment into the next smallest balance. Avalanche saves more money; snowball can be easier to stick with psychologically. Either way, the mechanism is the same: keep making minimum payments on every debt to avoid penalties, and concentrate all surplus on one target debt at a time rather than spreading it thin.
